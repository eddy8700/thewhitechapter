Our Story

Six years of looking. One quiet valley.

The White Chapter is the answer to a question we kept asking ourselves: where in these mountains can we just be still?

For six years, we wandered. Through Spiti and Kinnaur, through the upper reaches of Kullu, through villages most maps don't bother to mark. Every place was either too crowded, too compromised, or too far from the silence we were chasing.

Then we found Sissu — a small Lahauli village across the Atal Tunnel, ringed by white snow-peaked mountains, with a glacier-fed waterfall a short drive away. No traffic. No noise. Just wind through the pines, and the slow shift of light across the peaks.

The retreat is rising slowly. We are not in a hurry. We want this place to feel inevitable when you arrive — like it always belonged here, like it was waiting for you.

Abhishek Jain & Aditya Gupta Founders & Hosts

Experiences

The valley, just outside your window

Sissu sits in the middle of the most dramatic stretch of Lahaul. Our hosts curate every outing around the rhythm of the season — from waterfall walks in summer to frozen-lake mornings in deep winter.

01

Sissu Waterfall

About a 20-minute drive from our gate. The glacier-fed cascade thunders all summer; in winter, it freezes into a sculpture of blue ice.

02

Sissu Lake

A man-made lake beside Sissu Falls on the Chandrabhag river — turquoise water ringed by snow peaks, with boating in summer. Quieter once the season turns, the water stilling to glass.

03

Stargazing & Bonfire

Lahaul has some of the clearest skies in the country, and our own terrace is built for it. Step up after dinner for the stars, or gather round the bonfire — woollen throws and butter tea, with a host on hand for the constellations.

04

Sissu Monastery

Also known locally as Labrang Gompa, this small, peaceful Buddhist shrine sits in Labrang village just above Sissu — modern in build, with statues of the Buddha in his many forms and a clear view down to the Chandra river.
